Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

What the app accesses

NicheDesk Analytics uses Google sign-in and read-only access to the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the YouTube Reporting API. With the channel owner's permission it reads statistics of that owner's channels: views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions, click-through rate and estimated revenue. It also reads the email address of the Google account that gave permission, to keep track of which account each channel is connected under.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner reports about their own channels. The app does not upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and collected statistics are stored on the owner's own computer. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

NicheDesk Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

You can revoke the app's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. After that the app can no longer read the channel's data.

Third-party terms

The app uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
